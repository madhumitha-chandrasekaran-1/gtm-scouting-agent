# GTM Opportunity Scout — Report for Madhumitha
**Run date:** September 17, 2026
**Scope:** Early-stage US startups with recent funding/YC momentum, small teams, and GTM hiring signals (growth/sales/BD/founder's associate roles).

---

## Methodology note

I ran separate searches across funding news (TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, AlleyWatch, Axios Pro Rata attempts, sector-specific press), YC's directory and Work at a Startup, a16z's jobs newsletter, and Ashby/Lever/Wellfound/Greenhouse job boards for target role titles. I verified every company below against at least one primary or named source before including it. **I did not pad the list to hit 15** — several promising leads (e.g., AllSpice.io, Nuance Labs, Bluecore Energy, Tessellate) turned up in searches but failed verification on either funding recency, a confirmed GTM hiring signal, or US HQ, so they're either excluded or moved to "worth watching" with the specific gap flagged. Two companies (Clipbook, Felicity) have funding rounds older than the 90-day window; I kept them because they still meet most other criteria and have a live, verifiable GTM role open — flagged clearly in the table.

Some data points (exact team size at a point in time, exact funding-close dates for less-covered raises) vary slightly across sources (Crunchbase/PitchBook/Tracxn vs. press releases). Where sources conflicted, I used the company's own blog/press release or the most recent press coverage and noted it.
